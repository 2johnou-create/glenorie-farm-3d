How to read the model

  • The property includes Lots 5, 6 and 7 in DP737144. Boundaries, existing building footprints and survey levels provide the wider site context.
  • The compass shows MGA grid north, rather than true north.
  • Whole property includes Lot 7’s 3.5 m wide access strip. Its off-sheet end is located from survey dimensions.
  • The café, staff shed/store, paths and driveway are P9 proposals.
  • The simplified café shell carries forward the prior proposed 25 × 14.5 m building model around the surveyed 20 × 10 m shed footprint.
  • Survey heights are carried into an interpolated terrain surface. Ground between measured levels is illustrative.
  • Tree species and heights, and building heights, are indicative where the survey does not specify them.
  • The dam’s displayed water level is illustrative.
  • Contour lines follow the survey’s traced plan positions; displayed heights follow the interpolated terrain rather than individually verified contour elevations.
  • Buildings use simplified forms so their relationship to the land and routes remains clear.
