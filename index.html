Building the café…

Plan to model

Shell dimensions follow The Shed Company A.05–A.09. Interior zones and operations follow your six-page New Design v2_P1 revision and the updated New Design v2_P6 sheet supplied on 4 October 2026. This is a spatial design concept for review.

Dimensions carried into the model

  • Overall building: 25 m long; 10 m existing shed depth plus 4.5 m extension. Portal grid: five 5 m bays.
  • Commercial area: 15 × 14.5 m. Kitchen: 4 × 6.5 m. Toilet block: 6 × 4 m. Glass roastery: 5 × 4 m.
  • Existing shed: 4.00 m eaves and 4.88 m ridge. Extension: 3.33 m outer eave and 3.80 m high edge.

Your revised arrangement

  • Side entrance through the open entry bay; large farm-facing glazing; operable stock gate.
  • Enclosed kitchen with service window and separate exit through a small two-door lobby.
  • Toilet block with cleaning store, unisex room, accessible-room concept and glass sliding entrance.
  • Glass roastery with schematic 6 kg and 1.5 kg machines.
  • Fixed curved counter and 3 m feature wall share one concrete-style render. The toilet sliding-glass entrance remains within the feature wall.
  • One 3 m indoor feature tree, with four removable product-display tables around it. The tree’s height includes its planter.
  • Mobile workshop tables and 24 chairs, produce shelves and window seats.
  • Concealed 1.8 m staff gate to farm storage, interpreted as pallet-width access.

Assumptions to review

  • Tree species, canopy shape, wall thickness and the counter-to-wall junction are concept interpretations of P6. Tree and feature wall remain in place when furniture is cleared.
  • Room dimensions take priority over the approximate proportions in the marked-up sketch. Door sizes, counter geometry, fixture positions, finishes and partition heights are concept estimates.
  • The south extension eave is labelled 3.30 m; other sheets use 3.33 m. A 3.33 m outer edge is used here. Roof joins, flashings and portal sections are simplified; the existing roof is interpreted as a gable.
  • Drawings show differing floor RLs: 174.000 in elevations and 173.250 in sections. The model uses one relative floor datum at 0.00 m. Slab depth and flat ground are illustrative.
  • Existing/internal posts are retained and some minor-post locations are traced approximately. Column removal is unresolved and needs engineering design before changing this model.
  • Toilet ventilation, kitchen systems, roastery extraction, access and egress need detailed coordination; the geometry does not establish compliance. No council approval status has been verified.
  • Movable window seats are supplementary furniture; the displayed 24 count refers to workshop chairs only.
